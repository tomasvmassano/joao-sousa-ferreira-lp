Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

How much does it cost?

The first strategy session is free. If it makes sense to move forward with ongoing legal support, we present a clear, transparent proposal tailored to the size and needs of your company. No hidden contracts, no fine print.

Is the session really free?

Yes. The initial strategy session is 100% free and with no commitment whatsoever. It's an opportunity for us to understand your company, identify the main legal challenges, and — if it makes sense for both sides — propose the best way to work together.

How long does it take?

Our first contact happens within one business day of submitting the form. The strategy session typically lasts 45 to 60 minutes. If you decide to move forward with ongoing support, we start work immediately after signing the proposal.

Am I required to hire you?

No. The strategy session is free and comes with no obligation to hire. If our proposal doesn't fit or you'd rather go a different route, there's no mutual commitment.

Do you work across the country?

Yes. Our office is in Oeiras, in the Lisbon district, but we work with companies throughout Portugal. Sessions and regular meetings happen via video call (Google Meet); in-person meetings in Lisbon when it makes sense.

How does the ongoing support work?

Support adapts to each company. It typically includes:

  • Regular availability for legal questions
  • Contract review and negotiation
  • Support on strategic and corporate decisions
  • Prevention of disputes with employees, clients or suppliers
  • Representation in dealings with third parties

The proposal is always designed around the size and stage of your business, with fixed and predictable fees.
