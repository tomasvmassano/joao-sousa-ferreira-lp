About SFBA

At Sousa Ferreira, Borges, Amaral — Law Firm (SFBA), we believe legal support should create value for the business, not just resolve problems after they've already arisen.

We work side by side with Portuguese SMEs, supporting their decisions, preventing risk, structuring the business, and ensuring the law works as a tool for protection and growth.

More than answering legal questions, we help our clients make decisions with greater confidence and peace of mind.

About João Sousa Ferreira

Attorney and partner at SFBA, with a practice centred on ongoing legal support for companies.

Over the past few years he has helped entrepreneurs at every stage of their business's growth, from initial structuring to managing commercial relationships, corporate and labour matters, debt recovery, and dispute prevention.

The goal is simple: help companies grow with the peace of mind of having a legal partner who knows their business and is available whenever needed.

Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

How much does it cost?

The first session is entirely free. If, at the end, we conclude it makes sense to work together, we'll present a proposal tailored to your company's needs — with no commitment.

Is the strategy session free?

Yes. The purpose of the session is to understand your company's reality, identify the main legal challenges and determine whether we can effectively help you.

How long does it take?

The form takes about 1 minute to fill in. Once you submit, our team reviews the request and gets in touch. If there's a fit, we'll schedule a strategy session of approximately 30 minutes.

Am I required to hire SFBA?

No. The strategy session comes with no commitment. We only present a proposal if we believe we can add value to your business — and only move forward if you choose to work with us.

Do you work with companies from anywhere in Portugal?

Yes. We support companies throughout the country, in person or via video call, according to each client's needs.

How does the ongoing support work?

After the strategy session, if you decide to move forward, we define the solution best suited to your company. Our goal is to act as a long-term legal partner — supporting your business's growth, preventing risk and backing the most important decisions.

What type of companies do you support?

We work primarily with small and medium-sized enterprises looking to grow sustainably and who value close, strategic and continuous legal support.

My matter is urgent. Can I be contacted quickly?

Yes. Once we receive your request, we review it as promptly as possible. If the situation is urgent, please indicate it in the form so we can prioritise accordingly.
