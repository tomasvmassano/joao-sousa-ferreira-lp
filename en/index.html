Typical cases

Legal problems rarely start in court. They start here.

  1. 01 Real Estate

    You're about to buy a property and want to know if there are any issues with the documentation.

  2. 02 Real Estate

    You're abroad and want to buy a property in Portugal.

  3. 03 Real Estate

    You received a letter from your landlord or tenant and aren't sure how — or what — to reply.

  4. 04 Real Estate

    You have a property to place on the rental market and want to minimize the risks of letting it out.

  5. 05 Inheritance

  6. 06 Inheritance

    One heir is using the estate's assets alone and refuses to hand over the property.

    You need regular legal support and predictable costs.

  8. 08 Business

    Your clients refuse or avoid paying outstanding amounts for goods or services provided.

Frequently asked questions

What you need to know.

How much does the consultation cost and how is payment made?

The consultation costs €100.00 + VAT and payment is made after the session against the issuance of the corresponding invoice.

Is the consultation online or in-person?

The consultation can be either online (via Google Meet) or in-person, at the SFBA office located in Oeiras, in the Lisbon district.

Cancellation and refund policy?

Rescheduling or cancellation may be made free of charge up to 24 hours before the consultation. Cancellations after this period are invoiced in full.

What if I miss the consultation?

Free rescheduling or cancellation can be made up to 24 hours before the consultation. If you miss the consultation, the corresponding invoice will be issued and sent to you for payment.

Is the consultation confidential?

Yes. All information shared during the consultation is protected by the attorney's professional secrecy duty. The documents and details of your case will be handled with full confidentiality.

What if my case is too complex for 1 hour?

The consultation is designed to analyse the case, identify the main risks, and define the next steps. If the situation requires further follow-up or additional work, a clear and standalone proposal will be presented for that purpose.

How long does the consultation last?

The legal consultation lasts 1 hour.

What documents should I bring?

Anything relevant to the matter you'll be presenting: contracts, notifications, emails, letters, photos.

Which languages are available?

Portuguese, English or Spanish.

Will I speak directly with Mr. João Sousa Ferreira or with another attorney?

Consultations are always conducted by Mr. João Sousa Ferreira.

Does the consultation guarantee my case will be accepted?

No. It serves to legally assess the case and indicate the next steps.

Does the consultation replace a fee proposal?

Not necessarily. If further follow-up is required, a standalone fee proposal will be presented.

Can I send documents in advance?

Yes, to better prepare the consultation.

What if the case is urgent?

The urgency will be assessed and the most suitable procedural route indicated.

Does the consultation include court representation?

The consultation is intended for case analysis and defining the next steps. If it becomes necessary to proceed with judicial proceedings or further follow-up, a standalone proposal tailored to the specific case will be presented.
